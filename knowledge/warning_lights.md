# Warning lights (sample indicator meanings)

## Read this first
These are sample indicator meanings for training in this prototype. The real meaning of each light is in the machine's own operation manual; always check it there.

## Red lights
A red light means stop safely as soon as possible. Examples: low engine oil pressure, high coolant temperature, hydraulic oil too hot. Park, lower the attachment, switch off and call maintenance.

## Amber lights
An amber light means attention needed soon. Examples: air filter restricted, water in fuel, service due. Finish the current task safely and report it.

## Blue or green lights
These show that a function is on, such as work lights or the auto-idle feature. No action is needed.
