# Reporting incidents and near misses

## What to report
Report every incident, where someone was hurt or something was damaged, and every near miss, where it almost happened. Report machine problems too. Near misses are the cheapest lessons: reporting them prevents the next accident.

## How to report
Open Report on the cab screen, or hold the talk button and say what happened. ShiftMate fills in the time, machine, place, task and weather for you. Check the draft, change anything that is wrong, then send it.

## No internet
Reports are saved on the machine and sent when the internet is back. The report list shows which ones are still waiting.
