# Tiredness and breaks

## Signs of tiredness
Yawning, heavy eyes, slow reactions, missing signals and drifting thoughts are signs you need a break. Tired operators make the same mistakes as operators who have been drinking.

## When ShiftMate reminds you
ShiftMate reminds you to plan a short break after about two and a half hours of continuous work, and asks you to stop for a break after four hours. When the heat or the risk level is high, these reminders come sooner.

## Taking a good break
Park safely, switch off and get out of the cab. Drink water, stretch and rest your eyes away from screens for a few minutes.
