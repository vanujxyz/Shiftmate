# Walkaround inspection

## When to do it
Walk all the way around the machine before the first start of every shift, and again after any long stop where the machine was left unattended. The check takes about five minutes. Do it with the engine off and the key removed.

## What to look at
Tracks or tyres: no cuts, missing pads or loose parts. Under the machine: no puddles of oil, fuel or coolant. Lights, beacon and horn work. Mirrors and cameras are clean and set. The seatbelt has no cuts or fraying and the buckle latches firmly. The fire extinguisher is in place and charged. Nobody is working around the machine.

## If you find a problem
Answer "Problem" on the checklist. ShiftMate turns it into a report for your supervisor. Do not operate a machine with a fault that affects steering, brakes, the seatbelt or visibility until it is repaired or the supervisor has said it is safe.
