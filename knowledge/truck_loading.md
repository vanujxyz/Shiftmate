# Loading trucks

## Setting up
Position the machine so the truck can be loaded from the side or the back, never over the truck's cab. Keep the swing short: a truck at 90 degrees or less from the dig face saves time and fuel on every cycle.

## Loading the truck
Spread the load evenly from the front of the body to the back, and do not overload. Lower the bucket close to the body before releasing the load so material does not hit the truck hard. Sound the horn when the truck is full.

## Waiting for trucks
If no truck is at the loading point, stop the machine in a safe position and switch off for waits longer than five minutes. Truck waits are logged as a site delay, not as your idle time.
