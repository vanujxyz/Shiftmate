# Working with a spotter

## Before you start
Agree on the hand signals with the spotter before work begins, and agree that a closed fist raised high means stop. The spotter stands where you can see them and away from the swing radius and the path of the machine.

## Common signals
Stop: arm raised, fist closed. Emergency stop: both arms waved from side to side above the head. Move towards me: palm towards the body, beckoning. Move away: palm out, pushing away. Swing: arm pointing in the direction of the swing.

## If you lose sight of the spotter
Stop immediately and do not move until you can see the spotter again. Never follow a signal from someone who is not the agreed spotter, except a stop signal, which you always obey.
