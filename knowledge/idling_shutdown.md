# Idling and shutting down

## Why switching off matters
An engine left idling burns fuel, adds engine hours and wear, and makes noise and fumes, without moving any material. For stops longer than five minutes, switching off saves fuel.

## Shutting down correctly
Park on firm, level ground away from edges. Lower the bucket or blade to the ground. Let a hot engine idle for a few minutes to cool down after heavy work, then switch off and remove the key.

## Leaving the cab
Never leave the cab with the engine running. ShiftMate treats an engine running with the seat empty for more than two minutes as an unattended machine, which is a safety event.
