# Emergency stop and leaving the cab

## Emergency stop
In an emergency, stop all movement, lower the attachment to the ground if you can do so safely, and switch off the engine. Sound the horn to warn people nearby.

## Leaving the cab in an emergency
If you must get out, use the main door if it is safe. If the door is blocked, use the emergency exit window and the hammer kept in the cab. Face the machine and keep three points of contact while climbing down.

## Fire
If there is a fire, switch off the engine, get out and move well away, upwind. Use the fire extinguisher only on a small fire and only if you have a clear way out. Call the supervisor at once.
