# Seatbelt and rollover protection

## Why the seatbelt matters
The cab is built to protect you if the machine tips or rolls over, but only if you stay inside it. Without the seatbelt you can be thrown out and crushed by the machine. Most rollover deaths happen to operators who were not wearing a seatbelt.

## When to wear it
Fasten the seatbelt before you start the engine and keep it on whenever the machine moves or works. If ShiftMate sees the machine working with the seatbelt unfastened, it raises a critical alert. Stop, buckle up, then carry on.

## Checking the seatbelt
At every walkaround, check the belt for cuts and fraying and check that the buckle latches and releases. A damaged seatbelt must be replaced before the machine is used. Never tie, cut or bypass the seatbelt or its switch.
