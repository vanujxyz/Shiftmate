# Cold start and warm-up

## Why warm up
Oil moves slowly when the engine is cold. A short warm-up lets oil reach every part before the engine and hydraulics are loaded.

## How long
In normal weather, about five minutes of warm-up is enough; below zero degrees allow up to fifteen minutes. The engine is ready for full work when the coolant reaches about 60 degrees. Longer warm-up only wastes fuel.

## During warm-up
Run the engine at low speed and gently work each hydraulic function through its full range to warm the oil. ShiftMate counts this time as warm-up, not idle time.
