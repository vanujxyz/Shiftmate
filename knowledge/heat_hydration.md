# Heat stress and water breaks

## Signs of heat stress
Headache, dizziness, feeling sick, heavy sweating or no sweating at all, cramps and confusion are signs of heat stress. Heat stress slows reactions and causes mistakes before you feel unwell.

## Water breaks
Drink water often, not only when thirsty: a cup every 15 to 20 minutes in hot weather. When the heat index is 41 degrees or more and you have worked an hour without a break, ShiftMate suggests a water break. In high heat, break reminders also come sooner.

## If someone is unwell
Stop work, move them to shade, give water if they are awake, loosen tight clothing and call the supervisor. Confusion or fainting is an emergency: get medical help at once.
