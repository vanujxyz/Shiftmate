# Refuelling safely

## Before refuelling
Park on level ground, lower attachments and switch the engine off. No smoking and no open flames within the refuelling area.

## While refuelling
Stay with the nozzle the whole time and do not overfill. Wipe up any spill at once and keep the fuel cap clean so dirt does not enter the tank.

## After refuelling
Close the cap firmly. Report any leak from the tank, hoses or fittings before starting work.
