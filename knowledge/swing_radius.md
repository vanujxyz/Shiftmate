# Swing radius and exclusion zones

## The danger zone
The swing radius is the circle the back of the house and the boom sweep through when the machine turns. A person inside it can be struck or crushed against another object, and the operator often cannot see them. Keep the swing radius clear of people at all times.

## ShiftMate warning distances
For an excavator the usual warning distances are 10 m for caution, 6 m for danger and 3.5 m for stop. When the risk level is raised or high, these distances grow, so warnings come earlier. The Reach on the status rail shows where the person is relative to your swing.

## What to do when someone is close
If a person comes inside the danger ring, slow down and do not swing towards them. If someone is inside the swing radius, stop all movement, lower the bucket and wait until they are clear. Use the horn and, if there is a spotter, agree signals before work starts.
