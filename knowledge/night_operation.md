# Working at night

## Lights and visibility
Check all work lights and the beacon before dark. Clean the windows, mirrors and cameras: dust scatters light and hides people. Switch the cab screen to the night theme so it does not dazzle you.

## Seeing people
People are much harder to see at night, even in high-visibility clothing. Slow down, and trust the proximity warnings, but always look before you swing or travel.

## Fatigue at night
Night work makes tiredness come sooner. Take breaks when they are due, and tell the supervisor if you feel too tired to work safely.
