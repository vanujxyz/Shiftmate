# Working on wet and muddy ground

## Why it takes longer
Wet and muddy ground gives less grip, material sticks to the bucket and trucks move slowly. ShiftMate's estimates add time for wet ground so the plan stays honest.

## Staying stable
Keep away from trench edges and soft shoulders, which can collapse after rain. Travel slowly with the bucket low. On slopes, travel straight up or down, never across.

## After heavy rain
Walk the work area before starting if you can do it safely, and look for standing water, washed-out edges and soft spots. Tell the supervisor about ground that looks unsafe.
